# Oracle WebGL device-lab evidence

## Scope and honest status

This workspace has no connected iPhone, Android handset, or XR host. The
captures generated by `scripts/oracle-device-capture.mjs` are therefore
**browser-emulation evidence**, not real-device evidence. They are useful for
checking the capture procedure and the app's exposed scene telemetry, but they
do not satisfy the hardware-specific Safari/WebGL acceptance claim by
themselves.

Run the repeatable fallback capture from the workspace root:

```bash
PUPPETEER_EXECUTABLE_PATH=$(which chromium) \
  node artifacts/surrogate-oracle/scripts/oracle-device-capture.mjs
```

Outputs are written to `artifacts/surrogate-oracle/device-captures/`:

- `iphone-safari-entry.png` and `iphone-safari-sustained-conversation.png`
- `android-chrome-entry.png` and `android-chrome-sustained-conversation.png`
- `xr-entry.png` and `xr-sustained-conversation.png`
- one JSON record per profile with browser/device, scene phase, portrait
  reveal state, XR bridge status, WebGL status, render tier, GLB/particle
  telemetry, and page errors

The JSON records deliberately say `captureMode: "browser-emulation"` and
`physicalDeviceEvidence: false`. Do not change those fields when attaching
these files to a review.

## Physical capture checklist

Complete each row on the named physical device and attach screenshots or a
screen recording beside the matching row. Open the deployed/preview URL with
`?devui` so the scene phase and XR status are visible in the diagnostic HUD.

| Device/browser | Entry | Sustained conversation | Portrait reveal | White rectangular wall | GLB avatar | Particle field | Voice | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| iPhone Safari | not available in workspace | not available | not available | not observed | not confirmed | not confirmed | not confirmed | BLOCKED: no iPhone attached |
| Android Chrome | not available in workspace | not available | not available | not observed | not confirmed | not confirmed | not confirmed | BLOCKED: no Android attached |
| XR-capable host/device | not available in workspace | not available | not available | not observed | not confirmed | not confirmed | not confirmed | BLOCKED: no XR host attached |

For every physical run, record:

1. exact device model, OS, browser and browser version;
2. scene phase at entry and after the first Oracle response;
3. whether the portrait reveal was active;
4. whether XR mode and camera passthrough were active;
5. whether a white rectangular wall appeared before or after GLB
   materialization, including a screenshot if it did;
6. whether the GLB avatar stayed visible, particles continued moving, and
   voice played through at least two turns.

## Available fallback result

The emulated captures are retained as a desktop/WebGL fallback only. They
exercise the same entry path and record the live canvas context, render tier,
GLB transporter progress, particle counters, XR bridge status, and portrait
viewer state. SwiftShader may trigger the app's intentional render-tier
degradation; that is not evidence of a mobile GPU failure.
